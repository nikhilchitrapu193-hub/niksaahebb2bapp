Frequently asked questions

Who can order?

Any food business: restaurants, cloud kitchens, cafés, bakeries, hotels, caterers and canteens. Home chefs with an FSSAI registration are welcome too.

Is there a minimum order?

Each product has its own minimum quantity (shown on the product). There is no minimum order value.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery, or credit for approved partners — settle your balance weekly or monthly.

What if something arrives damaged or short?

Tell the driver at the door or contact us the same day. We adjust the invoice or replace the item on the next delivery.

Do you provide GST invoices?

Yes. Every order has a GST invoice with your GSTIN, available in your account.

Where do you source from?

Directly from farms, mills and brand distributors. Perishables are bought daily and stored cold.
